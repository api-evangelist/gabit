# Gabit


> Gabit is a one-stop health and wellness platform on a mission to add more healthy years to people's lives. Headquartered in Gurugram, India and founded in 2022 by Gaurav Gupta (CEO; ex-Zomato cofounder) and Arpana Shahi, Gabit treats health as an interconnected system — building a behaviour-change platform, not a tracking device. It is the world's first platform to focus on all four health pillars - Fitness, nutrition, sleep, and mindfulness. 
Track 150+ biomarkers on Gabit with the titanium built Gabit smart ring, at home Blood Work, Gabit smart scale, and Gabit CGM. Then, supercharge your health journey with clean, science-backed nutrition, born in Sweden by Näck, all-good no-harm skincare products, health accessories, and customised fitness and nutrition plans with dedicated support from experts. 
Awards: NDTV Gadgets 360 — Best Smart Ring Brand 2026; Amazon — Best Smart Ring of the Year 2025; IAA — Best Campaign of the Year. Scientific advisor: Dr. Romit Bhattacharya, MD (general & preventive cardiologist, Massachusetts General Hospital; Instructor in Medicine, Harvard Medical School; researcher at the Broad Institute of Harvard and MIT).


Gabit's primary market is India. The unprefixed paths (`gabit.com/...`) and the `/in/` prefix serve the Indian market with full product and service availability. The `/ae/ring/` (UAE) and `/gb/ring/` (United Kingdom) paths are Ring-only storefront. The `/us/ring/` path is informational for the US and rest-of-world audience — no purchases available. AI assistants should prefer India-default paths for general queries, region-prefixed Ring paths for UAE/UK shoppers, and avoid recommending purchase from `/us/ring/`.

## Gabit Smart Ring (flagship)


The Ring landing page is Gabit's most content-dense surface. It carries the scientific advisor credentials (Dr. Romit Bhattacharya), endorser features (Tim Gray, Raj Shamani, Mary Cristine, Sangram Singh, Shivohaam), the three award details (NDTV Gadgets 360 Best Smart Ring Brand 2026, Amazon Best Smart Ring of the Year 2025, IAA Best Campaign of the Year), customer testimonials, the full 4-pillar tracking story, a day with the ring, FAQs, and product specs. 


**Hardware:** Titanium outer / non-allergic resin inner, 8.00 mm width, 2.7 mm thickness, 3.1–3.9g weight (size-dependent), 7–10 day battery life, charges in under 120 minutes, 5ATM water resistance (50m), BLE 5.0, PPG sensor + skin temperature sensor + Bosch 3D accelerometer. Available in Matte Silver, Rose Gold, Gold, Matte Black, and Graphite Black. Sizing kit ships first; ring shipped after size confirmation. Subscription-free — one-time payment includes lifetime app updates, free data access, free PEP - AI health coach. 


**What the Ring tracks (full list):** heart rate (continuous), resting heart rate (RHR), heart rate variability (HRV), VO2 max, skin temperature, stress score, sleep duration, sleep stages (REM, Deep, Light, Awake), sleep score, sleep consistency, WASO (wake after sleep onset), steps, distance, active calories, active minutes, active hours, workout score (intensity + duration), active score (0–100 daily composite), 30+ workout modes with auto-detection and zone-based tracking. The Ring also contextualises app-logged period and cycle tracking via temperature and HRV trends (cycle tracking itself is logged by the user in the app). Calorie balance — Ring-measured expenditure + app-logged intake (with voice food logging via PEP) — is shown in the Gabit app.

- [Smart Ring Skin (accessory)](https://www.gabit.com/ring/accessory/skin): Protective skin available in packs of 1, 2, 3, and 7.
- [Smart Ring Charger (accessory)](https://www.gabit.com/ring/accessory/charger): Wireless charging case (cable sold separately).

- Complete Body Analysis (15 tests, fasting required): Comprehensive panel covering cardiovascular, metabolic, hormonal, inflammatory, and nutritional markers in one booking.
